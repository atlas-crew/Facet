## Description

Close the open-questions loop on the Identity Map: let users answer a generated open question in free text, have an LLM propose a concrete, layer-correct patch to the identity model (evidence → role bullet / skill; narrative → Self Model), and accept/edit/skip it. Answering applies the patch and marks the question resolved. Builds on the awareness generator + review editor (TASK-102.5) which only produces and edits questions today.
