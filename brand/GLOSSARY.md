# Facet Glossary

Definitions of brand terms. Use this when you need to look up exactly
what a term means, what to write instead of related terms, or how
terms relate to each other. Each entry includes good usage, what NOT
to substitute, and "See also" cross-refs.

For the broader vocabulary discussion (voice, register, do/don't
patterns), see [`COPY.md`](COPY.md). For predictable Q&A using these
terms, see [`FAQ.md`](FAQ.md).

---

## recut

*(verb)* — The brand verb. To take your existing identity model and
produce a precise face from it for a specific opportunity.
Structurally tied to the gem mark (you recut a stone),
methodologically tied to "correction over creation" (you don't
rewrite the model, you recut it), and categorically distinct from
competitor vocabulary.

- ✅ "Recut your resume for this opportunity"
- ✅ "Build once. Recut for each."
- ✅ "Same model, recut for what each moment needs"
- ❌ "Tailor your resume" (every resume tool says this — generic)
- ❌ "Generate a custom resume" (implies one-shot AI; Facet *refines*)
- ❌ "Optimize for the role" (empty utility verb)
- ❌ "Customize for each application" (generic)

**See also:** *face*, *model*, *substrate*.

## model

*(noun)* — The thing the user builds once. Structured deeply enough
to query and recompose, not just lay out. Always lowercase unless
starting a sentence. Pairs with verbs: *build the model*, *the model
sharpens*, *recut the model*.

- ✅ "Build a deep model of who you are professionally"
- ✅ "Each pass sharpens the model"
- ❌ "Your profile" (LinkedIn-flavored, suggests presentation layer)
- ❌ "Your career data" (technical / database register)
- ❌ "Your candidate package" (recruiter-flavored)
- ❌ "Your career story" (closer, but story is the interpretation
  layer, not the substrate)

**See also:** *substrate*, *recut*, *Career Operating System*.

## face / cut

*(noun)* — A presentation produced by recutting the model. Resume.
Cover letter. LinkedIn presentation. Recruiter card. Live-mode view.
Drills. Each face is a view of the same underlying model, filtered
for context. *Same diamond, different face.*

Use **"face"** or **"cut"** in copy where the brand metaphor matters
(landing page, marketing, manifesto). Use the artifact name
("Resume," "Cover Letter," etc.) in product UI and functional
documentation contexts.

- ✅ "Resume, cover letter, LinkedIn — each is a face of the model"
- ✅ "The cut for this opportunity emphasizes platform engineering"
- ❌ "Your tailored resume" (avoid *tailored*; the artifact is a face)

**See also:** *recut*, *model*.

## substrate

*(noun)* — The structured underside of the model. Six items:
**Problems · Solutions · Metrics · Technologies · Background ·
Narrative**. Pairs naturally:

- *Problems / Solutions* — what you've solved
- *Metrics / Technologies* — what you've measured, what you've used
- *Background / Narrative* — raw context vs synthesized story

The substrate is the part most career-search tools skip. They jump
straight to generating an artifact, because generating an artifact
demos well. Building the substrate takes time and feels like work.
It is work.

- ✅ "Identity is treated as a substrate, not a flat document"
- ✅ "The six-item substrate of problems, solutions, metrics, …"

**See also:** *model*, *Career Operating System*.

## vector

*(noun)* — A positioning angle. Examples: *Backend Engineering*,
*Security Platform*, *Distributed Systems Lead*, *Engineering
Leadership*. A vector tells the assembler which parts of the model
to surface for a given opportunity. One identity, many vectors.

Vectors are how Facet does *Vector Mapping* — the structural claim
that one model can serve many positioning angles without rewriting
yourself. The carousel, profile editor, and assembler all operate
around vectors.

- ✅ "Select a vector to recut for that angle"
- ✅ "Per-vector priority overrides"
- ❌ Don't conflate *vector* with *target* — a vector is the
  positioning angle, a target is the specific opportunity within
  that angle.

**See also:** *recut*, *model*.

## pass

*(noun)* — A 90-day usage window. Facet sells in passes rather than
subscription because career-search runs in bursts. A pass has a
12-month window to consume it; the model you build stays yours after
the pass closes.

- ✅ "Buy a pass when you start a search"
- ✅ "Your current pass expires in 14 days"
- ✅ "Between passes, the model is still there"
- ❌ Don't substitute *subscription* — the difference is structural,
  not branding. Subscription assumes ongoing usage; passes match the
  episodic rhythm of career-search.

**See also:** episodic-pass argument in
[`MANIFESTO.md`](MANIFESTO.md) "Career-search runs in bursts".

## search loop

*(retired)* — The previous term for Facet's iterative
search-and-prep cycle. Retired in favor of the diamond-centric
carousel narrative (intro → model → faces → refinement → close).
The source HTML for the old concept is preserved in
`brand/sheets/_archive/loop.html` for reference.

- ❌ Don't introduce *search loop* in new copy.
- ✅ When describing the iterative cycle in new contexts, reach for
  *the cycle* (per the methodology one-pager) or just describe the
  arc directly: *build the model, recut for each opportunity, prep
  per round, debrief, sharpen*.

**See also:** [`brand/sheets/method.html`](sheets/method.html) for
the current cycle visual.

## Career Operating System

*(concept name; capitalized)* — The brand's structural claim: Facet
is the platform on which every career-search output runs from a
single identity model. Capitalized as a proper noun when invoking
the concept.

- ✅ "Facet's **Career Operating System** runs every output through
  a single model."
- ✅ "A career operating system for senior engineers" (descriptive
  use, lowercase, fine in product / hero copy)
- ❌ "the career operating system framework" — adding "framework"
  weakens the claim
- ❌ Don't confuse with **career-search operating system** (lowercase,
  hyphenated): a legacy hero variant retained for technical /
  dev-audience contexts. Don't use both in the same surface.
- ❌ "career platform" — banned (generic; see
  [`COPY.md`](COPY.md#what-not-to-use))

**See also:** *substrate*, *model*, and the
[`system.html`](sheets/system.html) concept sheet for the visual
treatment.
