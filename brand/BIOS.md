# Facet Bios & Boilerplate

Reusable copy chunks for press requests, partnership emails, conference
programs, podcast intros, "about" pages, and social profiles. Each piece
gets used dozens of times once written; the point is to write each
version once, in the right voice, so future use is copy-paste rather
than re-invention.

Companion to [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md) (visual reference),
[`COPY.md`](COPY.md) (language reference, locked vocabulary), and
[`MANIFESTO.md`](MANIFESTO.md) (long-form positioning).

**Voice constraint:** all copy here uses the locked vocabulary from
[`COPY.md`](COPY.md): `recut` (not tailor / generate / customize),
`model` (not profile / career data), `substrate`. Avoid the patterns in
COPY.md "what NOT to use" — every line should say something only Facet
can say.

**What's not here:** specific pricing numbers (those live in
`PRICING.md`); customer testimonials, case studies, or coverage clips
(none yet — pre-launch); founder location or biographical specifics
beyond what's verifiable.

## Notes for use

- **Founder + company boilerplate often pair.** Solo-founder context:
  the founder bio and company boilerplate frequently appear together
  ("Facet was built by Nick Ferguson, [founder bio]. The product,
  [company boilerplate]…"). Pick lengths that complement each other —
  matching word counts (50w + 50w, 250w + 250w) keeps rhythm; mismatched
  pairs (250w founder + 50w company) make one feel padded.
- **Don't quote past these.** If a publication needs a *different* word
  count or *different* phrasing, write it custom rather than tweaking
  these. The whole point is that these versions stay stable across
  reuse.
- **Verify before press.** Before any pre-launch piece goes public,
  spot-check that the launch-state language ("currently preparing for
  public launch") still matches reality. After launch, swap to the
  appropriate state.
- **Em-dash policy.** The 50w boilerplate uses a connective em-dash
  (display-compressed copy, allowed per
  [`COPY.md`](COPY.md#voice-and-register)). The 250w versions avoid
  connective em-dashes (long-form prose rule). The founder bios mix
  registers; revisit if rewriting.
